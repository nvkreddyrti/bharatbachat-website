The Journey of Bharat Bachat

The journey of Bharat Bachat began in 2010 when our founder, Neelam Vamsi Krishna Reddy, started his career as a laborer. Witnessing labor exploitation and financial struggles firsthand, he dedicated his life to creating a solution that makes wealth creation accessible to every Indian.

After demonstrating perseverance as a production manager and a bike taxi gig worker, Neelam realized that small, consistent contributions—like ₹3-₹5 per ride—could compound into significant wealth. This insight led to the birth of Desipe Finance and Bharat Bachat.
